EXCLUSIVE: ‘Humor is good,’ Union Pacific CEO says of video rate threat

Logistic Wave Intelligence Desk Omaha, Nebraska / Freight Intelligence Desk Published 12 November 2026, 08:30 EST
Share
LISTEN TO THIS ARTICLE
3 mins • AI voice
1.0×

Union Pacific's chief executive addressed controversy surrounding a leaked internal video in which rate escalation was discussed, telling stakeholders that the remarks were meant humorously rather than as a contractual ultimatum to captive shippers.

The comments, which triggered scrutiny from the Surface Transportation Board (STB) and heightened vigilance among national manufacturing coalitions, arrive at a sensitive juncture for transcontinental rail freight operations. Rail shippers have increasingly voiced concerns over precision scheduled railroading (PSR) service metrics, demurrage tariffs, and unilateral price adjustments across Midwestern agricultural and intermodal routes.

"Rate negotiations must reflect real operating velocity, network capital investment, and mutual contractual trust. Market discipline is paramount as supply chains rebalance toward domestic corridors."
— Logistic Wave Strategic Rail Briefing

Federal regulators indicated they will review the underlying video documentation to assess whether market concentration along non-competitive corridors has influenced freight rate determination. Shippers represent over $90 billion in annual commodity movements across the rail network.
